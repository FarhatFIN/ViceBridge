# Roadmap

0. Audit and reproducible build — in progress.
1. Loader, TLS, imports and exception handling — blocked by startup crash; reproduce with synthetic fixture.
2. Memory, synchronization and audio — establish ABI and completion contracts.
3. Graphics and resource upload — establish title-memory ownership and native layouts.
4. First real game frame — require traceable GPU capture and reproducible launch.
5. World rendering — validate geometry, textures, lighting and frame sequence.
6. Stability and performance — long runs and profiling after correctness.

Completion requires passing relevant tests and observing the stated behavior; synthetic results do not establish gameplay.
